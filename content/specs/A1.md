The low-pass filter is a 12 dB/octave biquad, Q 1 unless `.lpq()` sets it. `.lpenv(n)` opens the cutoff n octaves above `.lpf()` on each note with the filter envelope's defaults attack 0.005 s, decay 0.14 s, sustain 0, release 0.1 s (a pluck). A synth note with no envelope control set uses attack 0.001, decay 0.05, sustain 0.6, release 0.01; set only `.decay()` and the sustain falls to nothing (a pluck); set only `.attack()` or only `.release()` and the sustain is 1. `.delaytime()` is in seconds (at 100 BPM a beat is 0.6, an eighth 0.3, three quarters of a beat 0.45); left out, it is 3/16 of a bar, which at 100 BPM is 0.45; `.delayfeedback()` defaults to 0.5 and is capped at 0.98. `.roomsize()` is the reverb's length, about 2 when left out. Every orbit has ONE reverb and ONE delay: `.room()` and `.delay()` are each part's send, but the room size, delay time and feedback are the orbit's, set by whichever note wrote them last; `.orbit(2)` gives a part its own.

## A1.L01 Waves & Harmonics

Author: **spec-A1**. Key C major, 4/4, 100 BPM. A side lesson: outside the default order, offered from its gate on, written for a learner at the gate.

**Objective (syllabus).** The harmonic series; see a wave in Oak's scope panel.

**The one idea.** Every pitched note is a stack of sine waves at 1, 2, 3, 4 … times its frequency, the harmonic series, and a wave's colour is which harmonics it has and how loud: a sine only the first, a square and a triangle the odd ones, a sawtooth all of them. `.scope()` shows the shape; `.partials([…])` chooses the harmonics.

**Sound first.** The A1 loop twice within 20 seconds: bass and riff on sines (thin, far away), then the same notes with the bass on a six-harmonic sawtooth and the riff on a square (full, close).

```js
setcpm(100/4)

kick: s("bd ~ bd ~")
snare: s("~ sd ~ sd")
hats: s("hh*8").gain(.5)
bass: note("<[c2 ~ c2 g1] [f1 ~ f1 c2]>").s("sawtooth").partials([1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1])
mel: n("<[4 4 2 4 ~ 2 0 ~] [5 5 3 5 ~ 3 0 ~]>").scale("C4:major").s("square").partials([1, 1, 1, 1, 1]).gain(.8)
```

**Skills.** Introduces: D02 Oscillators & harmonics (needs D01). Practises: none.

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** `term.frequency` "frequency" in S4: How many times a wave repeats each second, in hertz (Hz): A4 is 440, the A an octave below 220. `term.harmonic-series` "harmonic series" in S5: The pitches at 1, 2, 3, 4 … times a note's frequency, from the note itself up: which of them a sound has, and how loud, is its colour. A sine has only the first.

**Can-do.** `cd.code.harmonics` "I can see a wave's shape and hear its harmonics."

**Unlocked here (content/toolbox.ts).** `tb.scope` `tb.partials`

## A1.L02 Filters & Filter Envelopes

Author: **A1**. Key C major, 4/4, 100 BPM. A side lesson: outside the default order, offered from its gate on, written for a learner at the gate.

**Objective (syllabus).** Cutoff, resonance and movement.

**The one idea.** A low-pass filter lets a sound's low harmonics through and takes away those above its cutoff: lower the cutoff and the sound gets darker (`.lpf()`; `.hpf()` takes the lows away instead, `.bpf()` keeps a band). Resonance (`.lpq()`) adds a ringing peak at the cutoff, and a filter envelope (`.lpenv()` with `.lpa() .lpd() .lps() .lpr()`) moves the cutoff on every note: the pluck of a synth riff.

**Sound first.** The A1 loop as A1.L01 left it (a raw sawtooth bass, a bright riff), then with the bass under `.lpf(400).lpq(4)` and the riff plucked by a filter envelope; both within the first 20 seconds.

```js
setcpm(100/4)

kick: s("bd ~ bd ~")
snare: s("~ sd ~ sd")
hats: s("hh*8").gain(.5)
bass: note("<[c2 ~ c2 g1] [f1 ~ f1 c2]>").s("sawtooth").lpf(400).lpq(4)
mel: n("<[4 4 2 4 ~ 2 0 ~] [5 5 3 5 ~ 3 0 ~]>").scale("C4:major").s("sawtooth").lpf(300).lpenv(4).lpd(.2).gain(.8)
```

**Skills.** Introduces: D03 Filters & filter envelope (needs D02). Practises: none.

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** `term.filter` "filter" in S3: A tool that takes some frequencies out of a sound: a low-pass filter keeps the lows and darkens the sound. `term.cutoff` "cutoff" in S3: The frequency where a filter starts to cut: a lower cutoff on a low-pass filter makes a darker sound. `term.resonance` "resonance" in S5: A peak a filter adds at its cutoff, which makes the sound ring or whistle.

**Can-do.** `cd.code.filters` "I can shape a sound with a filter and a filter envelope."

**Unlocked here (content/toolbox.ts).** `tb.lpf (at the start)` `tb.hpf` `tb.bpf` `tb.lpq` `tb.lpenv`

## A1.L03 Envelopes

## A1.L04 Space

Author: **A1**. Key C major, 4/4, 100 BPM. A side lesson: outside the default order, offered from its gate on, written for a learner at the gate.

**Objective (syllabus).** Reverb size, delay time and feedback, orbits.

**The one idea.** Space is reverb and delay. `.room()` sends a part to a reverb and `.roomsize()` sets how big the room is; `.delay()` sends it to an echo, `.delaytime()` sets the gap in seconds (60 ÷ BPM per beat) and `.delayfeedback()` how many repeats.
