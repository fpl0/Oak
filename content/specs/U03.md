Eleven lessons from 'this note is home' to an 8-bar melody that ends there. U03.L01 builds the major scale with note names; from U03.L02 every line is written in degrees (`n` = degree − 1 over `.scale("C4:major")`), the key becomes one parameter, the roll and staff label degrees, and the first functional ear-training family (1, 3, 5 after a few chords that set home) starts.
