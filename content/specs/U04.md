## Conventions for this unit

- An interval is counted in degrees first (1 up to 3 is a 3rd: count both ends), then measured in half steps (a major 3rd is 4, a minor 3rd 3). Write qualities as words in prose ("a major 3rd") and as Strudel's strings in code (`"3M"`, `"3m"`, `"5P"`); show both once: "`.transpose("3M")` (a major 3rd up)". Augmented and diminished are U05.L03's words except the tritone, which U04.L03 names.
- A second melody line is labelled `vox` (AUTHORING §13: a digit-suffixed `mel2` is not a melody to the grader yet). Notes played together use `,` only from U04.L03 (`tb.mini.stack`); before that, two voices are two labelled lines.
- `.transpose()` comes after note names, never after `.scale()`: the lint refuses `.transpose()` after `.scale()` (it means a key change; use a patterned `.scale()` for that). A harmony line a named interval away is written in note names: `note("c4 e4").transpose("3M")`.
- U04's "Play from the page" step is U04.L03 S4: four pairs stacked on the staff, staff only, written with `,` (course.test requires one per unit from U02).
- Every Make uses `band.two-chord-vamp` (I–IV) in C. Over its bar 1 (C–E–G) nothing is held a beat a half step above a chord note: no F over E, no C♯, no G♯; the tritone hook of U04.L03 plays without the Band.
- Singing is on syllables with numbers shown (U03.L08 on); `fam.int.sing` starts in U04.L04. Passive listening (U04.L04) is a `listen` step with a `passive` block: a stream of generated in-key pairs, each named as it plays; no answer, no evidence, no SRS.
- Feedback the grader emits on U04 code steps: `code.unchanged`, `code.must-use`, `melody.wrong-degree`, `melody.octave`, `melody.missing-note`, `harmony.pcset` (a chord with the wrong notes), `code.transpose-after-scale` (a lint warning when `.transpose()` follows `.scale()`), `spell.wrong`, `id.wrong`, `rubric.unmet`. ## U04.L01 Steps Apart

Author: **U04**. Key C major, 4/4, 92 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** Intervals inside a key: distance between degrees (2nd, 3rd … octave); two-voice riffs over the Band.

**The one idea.** The distance between two notes of a key is counted in degrees, both ends included: 1 to 3 is a 3rd, 1 to 5 a 5th, 1 to 1' an octave.

**Sound first.** A riff in two voices over the Band, the upper one always two degrees above: the sound of 3rds.

```js
setcpm(92/4)

mel: n("<[0 2 4 2] [3 5 4 ~]>").scale("C4:major").s("piano")
vox: n("<[2 4 6 4] [5 7 6 ~]>").scale("C4:major").s("piano").gain(.7)
```

## U04.L03 Together Now

**Objective (syllabus).** Harmonic intervals, consonance and dissonance, the tritone; `,` inside mini-notation.

**The one idea.** Two notes played together either rest or clash; 3rds, 6ths, 5ths and octaves rest, 2nds, 7ths and the tritone pull, and `,` plays notes together.

**Sound first.** Four pairs of notes played together, no Band: C–E, C–G, then B–F (the tritone), and back to C–E.

```js
setcpm(84/4)

keys: n("<[0,2] [0,4] [-1,3] [0,2]>").scale("C4:major").s("piano")
```

**Skills.** Introduces: I05 Consonance & dissonance (needs I02); I03 Tritone, augmented & diminished (needs I02). Practises: none.
