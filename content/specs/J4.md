The four lessons where the jazz track's chords become whole tunes (contracts §2.6). The turnaround that sends a tune back to its top, I–vi–ii–V and its brighter cousin iii–VI–ii–V (J4.L01); the twelve-bar blues the learner knows from U11 with jazz chords in it, ii–Vs into bars 5, 9 and the top, a diminished chord in bar 6 (J4.L02, the muted trumpet unlocks with it); rhythm changes, the 32-bar AABA on I Got Rhythm's chords, I–vi–ii–V turns in the A sections and a bridge of dominants a 4th apart (J4.L03); and a 1930 standard read from its lead sheet, head and changes (J4.L04). J4 opens after U15.L06 (secondary dominants by name, which the VI7 of a turnaround and the bridge of rhythm changes are) and may interleave with U16–U17; most learners reach it with the core finished. J4.L04 opens JC2, "Jazz Blues in F" (content/specs/JC2.ts). Every Make is a whole form the trio plays: a turnaround loop, a twelve-bar chorus, 32 bars of rhythm changes, a standard's A section.

- Every J4 tune is a form, and the form is the lesson: changes are written once (`const changes = "<…>"`, one entry a bar, two chords in a bar as `[Cm7 F7]`), and every line that plays chords reads them: the comping `chord(changes).dict("lefthand").anchor("a4").voicing()` (J3.L01) or `.dict("guidetones")`, the bass `chord(changes).rootNotes(2)` (two-feel) or a walk in note names (J3.L04). ## J4.L01 Turnarounds

Author: **J4a**. Key Eb major, 4/4, 132 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** I–vi–ii–V and iii–VI–ii–V.

**The one idea.** A turnaround is the last two bars of a tune sending it back to the top: I–vi–ii–V (E♭maj7 Cm7 Fm7 B♭7) is the one jazz shares with doo-wop; swap the first two chords for iii–VI (Gm7 C7) and the C7, a dominant aimed at Fm7, gives the turn a brighter, stronger pull: iii–VI–ii–V.

**Sound first.** Eight bars of the trio in E♭: four of I–vi–ii–V, then four of iii–VI–ii–V; the second time the C7 lifts the turn before it comes home.

```js
setcpm(132/4)

const changes = "<Eb^7 Cm7 Fm7 Bb7 Gm7 C7 Fm7 Bb7>"

drums: s("rd [rd rd] rd [rd rd]").swingBy(1/3, 4).bank("jazzkit")
hats: s("~ hh ~ hh").bank("jazzkit")
bass: chord(changes).rootNotes(2).s("upright")
chords: chord(changes).dict("lefthand").anchor("a4").voicing().struct("x ~ ~ x ~ ~ ~ ~").swingBy(1/3, 4).s("rhodes").gain(.6)
```

**Skills.** Introduces: J10 Turnarounds (needs J04, H12). Practises: none.

**Terms (≤ 3, a pair counts once).** none

## J4.L02 Jazz Blues in F

Author: **J4a**. Key F major, 4/4, 132 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** The jazz 12-bar.

**The one idea.** A jazz blues is the twelve bars the learner knows with jazz chords put in: bar 4 becomes a ii–V into bar 5 (Cm7 F7 into B♭7), bar 6 a diminished chord (B°7) that climbs to F7, bars 8 to 10 a chain of dominants and a ii–V (D7 Gm7 C7) into the last line, and bar 12 a turnaround (Gm7 C7) back to the top.

**Sound first.** A twelve-bar chorus in F by the trio and a muted trumpet: the ride and the hi-hat swinging, a walking bass, the Rhodes on guide tones on the Charleston, and a head on the trumpet that lands on a chord tone of every new chord.

```js
const changes = "<F7 Bb7 F7 [Cm7 F7] Bb7 Bo7 F7 D7 Gm7 C7 [F7 D7] [Gm7 C7]>"

drums: s("rd [rd rd] rd [rd rd]").swingBy(1/3, 4).bank("jazzkit")
hats: s("~ hh ~ hh").bank("jazzkit")
bass: note("<[f2 a2 c3 b2] [bb2 a2 ab2 g2] [f2 a2 c3 db3] [c3 eb3 f2 a2] [bb2 ab2 f2 c3] [b2 ab2 f2 e2] [f2 a2 c3 c#3] [d3 c3 a2 ab2] [g2 bb2 d3 db3] [c3 bb2 g2 gb2] [f2 a2 d2 f#2] [g2 bb2 c3 e2]>").s("upright")
```

## J4.L03 Rhythm Changes

Author: **J4b**. Key Bb major, 4/4, 176 BPM.

**Objective (syllabus).** The 32-bar AABA on I Got Rhythm.

**The one idea.** Rhythm changes are the 32-bar AABA chords of I Got Rhythm (1930): each A section is a string of I–vi–ii–V turns in B♭, and the bridge (B) is four dominant sevenths a 4th apart, two bars each, D7 G7 C7 F7, each one pulling to the next and the last back home to B♭. Half the bebop tunes ever written are new melodies on these chords.

**Sound first.** Thirty-two bars of rhythm changes in B♭ at a brisk 176: the turns of the A section twice, the bridge's chain of dominants, and the last A landing home.

```js
setcpm(176/4)

const A = "<[Bb^7 Gm7] [Cm7 F7] [Bb^7 Gm7] [Cm7 F7] [Fm7 Bb7] [Eb^7 Eo7] [Dm7 G7] [Cm7 F7]>"
const A3 = "<[Bb^7 Gm7] [Cm7 F7] [Bb^7 Gm7] [Cm7 F7] [Fm7 Bb7] [Eb^7 Eo7] [Cm7 F7] Bb^7>"
```

## J4.L04 Reading a Standard

**Objective (syllabus).** A public-domain head plus changes.
