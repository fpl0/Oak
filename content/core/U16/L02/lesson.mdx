<Step id="S3">
Here is the climb on its own, one chord a bar, slow enough to read.

<Example id="the-climb" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>A♭, B♭, C: three major chords, each root a whole step above the last. Both new chords come from C minor, like the sad IV. A♭ sits on the lowered 6th note and is written ♭VI; B♭ sits on the lowered 7th and is written ♭VII. You have played ♭VII before, in Mixolydian Rock. Here it arrives at home from a whole step below, with no leading note pulling up: that is why the ending sounds wide open rather than tight.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">

<OnPass>B♭, D, F: the major chord on ♭6 of D major. The key's own chord on 6 is B minor, B D F♯: two of its three notes differ from B♭ major, so ♭VI is not vi with one note moved.</OnPass>
