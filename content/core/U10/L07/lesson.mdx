<Example id="the-descent" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>i VII VI V, Am G F E. The roots walk down from home a step at a time, and the walk ends on the major V you made in the last lesson. VII keeps G natural; only V raises it to G♯. This descent is the **Andalusian cadence**: both minors in one loop, which is why it is written in chord names.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">

<OnPass>Am G F E: the descent now leans on G♯ and falls back into A each time round. You have the whole gesture in your hands, a good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
