<Step id="S3">
Three bars, three forms of minor, each from A to A. Watch the degree labels near the top of each bar.

<Example id="three-minors" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Harmonic minor raises 7 and leaves a gap of three half steps under it. Raise 6 as well, F to F♯, and the climb from 5 to home goes by steps: that is **melodic minor**. Tunes usually use it on the way up and come back down in natural minor, G then F.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
One scale per bar, the pattern you once used to change key. Here it changes the form of minor instead.

<Editor exercise="up-melodic-down-natural" />

<OnPass>E F♯ G♯ A up, A G F E down: raised going up, natural coming down. Your tune now climbs home the way a singer would, a good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
No code, just your ears. Listen to the notes just under home in each climb.

<Choice exercise="which-minor" />

<OnPass>Natural minor comes home from a whole step below, harmonic minor leaps into its raised 7, melodic minor climbs smoothly through both raised notes. Tomorrow's warm-up spells all three.</OnPass>
</Step>
