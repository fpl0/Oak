<Step id="S1">
Two sounds new to you, strings and an echo, on Am, F and E: the major V from Raise the Seventh, with its G♯. Then an old tune over the same chords.

<Step id="S2">
The tune's code and roll stay hidden; yours show as you write. Listen for home first, then the first note, then where the tune moves.

<Editor exercise="take-it-down" />

<OnPass>Sol below home, then up to me me re re, me me do: a minor tune you took down by ear, home on A with no chord needed to tell you so. A good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Your voice is analysed live on this computer. Nothing is recorded or sent.

The second line of the verse sings the same notes to new words, then answers with "let my people go". The key signature is empty, as in C major: the same notes, a new home. The one sharp is written on its note: G♯, the raised 7, ti, of Raise the Seventh. The chord names sit over the bars where they start: i, VI, V, i.

<Sing exercise="sing-from-the-page" />

<OnPass>Read and sung: a line that falls to home, and an answer that drops to sol and climbs through ti back to do.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
No code and no roll this time: only the page.

<Editor exercise="play-from-the-page" />

<OnPass>Sol sol ti ti do: the answer climbs through the raised 7 and lands home, the strongest arrival a minor key has. You have both halves of the line now.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
