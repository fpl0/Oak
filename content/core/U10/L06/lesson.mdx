<Example id="two-minors" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Natural minor with degree 7 raised a half step, from G to G♯, is **harmonic minor**. Its 7 is ti again, a half step below do, and it turns the chord on 5 into E major. The chord in bar 3 is why the lessons write these chords by name: stacked on ♭3 of harmonic minor, C E G♯ is augmented, not the C major that III should be.</OnPass>
