<Step id="S3">
In Major, Minor, Perfect you measured 3rds in half steps. A triad holds two of them: bottom to middle, middle to top.

<Example id="count-the-thirds" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>A up to C is three half steps, a minor 3rd; A up to C♯ is four, a major 3rd. The top 3rd changes the other way, so the outer 5th stays seven half steps. A major 3rd at the bottom with a minor 3rd on top is a **major triad**: bright and settled. A minor 3rd at the bottom with a major 3rd on top is a **minor triad**: darker, more inward.</OnPass>
</Step>
