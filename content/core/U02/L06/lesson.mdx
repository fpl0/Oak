<Choice exercise="same-tune" />

<OnPass>Same notes, different timbre: the colour of a sound you met in your first loop. The roll would draw both bars the same way; only the sound changed.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
`.s(…)` picks the sound. Besides the piano, Strudel has four basic synth sounds: `sine`, `triangle`, `square` and `sawtooth`.

<Callout kind="tip">Synths play at full strength with no fade, so they sound louder than the piano. A `.gain(.5)` keeps them from drowning the band.</Callout>

<OnPass>Each synth repeats one shape of vibration over and over, and that shape is its **waveform**. A sine is a smooth curve and sounds pure. A sawtooth ramps up and drops, and buzzes. A square jumps between two levels and sounds hollow; a triangle sits between sine and square, soft and round.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
No code and no roll this time. Four lines, four sounds, in any order.

<Choice exercise="name-the-sound" />

<OnPass>Four sounds told apart by ear, while the notes kept changing under them.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Arrangers double lines all the time: a synth an octave above a piano makes one line sound bigger without changing a note.

<Editor exercise="double-it-up" />

<OnPass>Two labelled lines, one line of music. The synth adds shine on top, and the piano still carries the tune.</OnPass>
</Step>
