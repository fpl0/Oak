<Step id="S1">
Each song you have made so far is one block of music: a loop, or eight bars that loop, then land. Most songs on your records have more than one block, and the blocks take turns. First one block on its own, then a song made of two.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Each block of each part is a named pattern, and one line per part says which block plays when. Read the plan before you hear it.

<Predict exercise="read-the-plan" />
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Run it and watch the moment the second block arrives.

<Example id="what-changes" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Bar 5 brings new chords, a tune a few steps higher and a tambourine, and bar 9 brings the first block back. The block that tells the story, whose music returns with new words, is the **verse**. The block that comes back with the hook and lifts the song is the **chorus**.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Every part that changes between the verse and the chorus needs its own plan, with the same sections in the same order.

<Editor exercise="arrange-the-bass" />

<OnPass>Now the bass follows the chords: C A F G in the verse, F G C C in the chorus. The plan of a song's sections, verse chorus verse chorus, or ABAB in letters, is its **song form**. That is a whole song, and a good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
A chorus feels bigger when something arrives with it. A part can rest for a whole section.

<Editor exercise="bring-something-in" />

<OnPass>The tambourine now waits for the chorus. New chords, a higher tune and one more sound: the chorus differs from the verse in three ways at once, and you hear it lift every time it comes round.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
The drums keep time. The verse is yours to keep; give it a chorus that answers it. Once the song runs verse, chorus, verse, chorus, the lift is yours: move the chorus tune up, bring a sound in, or give the bass more to do.

<Make exercise="give-it-a-chorus" />

<CanDo id="cd.code.arrange" />

You can lay out a song in sections now, one plan per part. Next: two more song shapes, and music of your own that comes only once.

<Recap />
</Step>
