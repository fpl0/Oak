<Step id="S2">
A sentence pauses at a comma and stops at a full stop. A tune does the same, and the chords under it agree.

<Example id="tune" views="code,roll,staff" />

<OnPass>Bar 4 rests on 5, over a chord that leans back towards home: a comma. Bar 8 lands on 1, over home's own chord: a full stop.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
A tune that stops anywhere but 1 sounds like a question nobody answered.

<Editor exercise="bring-it-home" />

<OnPass>Home in bar 8. Any way of getting there works, as long as the last note is 1.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
The second half of a tune often starts like the first. The ear hears the return and knows where it is.

<Editor exercise="start-again" />

<OnPass>Bar 5 starts the second half the way bar 1 started the first. This time, bars 7 and 8 take it home.</OnPass>
</Step>
