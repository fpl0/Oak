<Step id="S1">
A tune you may already know, in 3, and it starts before the first full bar. This time the music is on a page: notes on the staff, chord names above them.

<LeadSheet example="hook" />
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Everything a player needs is on one page. Read it the way a band player would: the tune along the staff, the chords from the names above.

<LeadSheet example="read-the-page" />

<OnPass>B♭ over bar 3, and a pickup of one beat. A song on one page, the tune on a staff with the chord symbols above it, is a **lead sheet**. It is how most pop, jazz and soul songs are passed between players: one page, and everyone plays their own part from it.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Play from the page. No code for the tune and no roll: only the lead sheet. Long notes fill two of the three beats; the two short notes in bar 1 share one beat.

<LeadSheet example="the-page" />

<Editor exercise="play-from-the-page" />

<OnPass>A rest, a rest, then C on the last beat of bar 0; then F held for two beats and A F in one. You read the pickup, the half notes and the eighths straight off the page.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Now sing it from the same page. After the count-in, keep counting 1, 2, 3 through the pickup bar, and come in on its 3.

<Sing exercise="sing-from-the-page" />

<Callout kind="tip">No microphone, or Quiet switched on: read the page and hear the tune in your head, then play Oak's version and compare.</Callout>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
The tune is written. The page tells you the chords too; write them so the tune has its harmony.

<LeadSheet example="the-symbols" />

<Editor exercise="chords-from-the-symbols" />

<OnPass>F, F, B♭, F, with nothing under the pickup. The symbols name the chords; how you voice them is yours, stacked by hand or by `.voicing()`. Both read the same off the page.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
One more page, in another key. Read a single bar of it.

<LeadSheet example="one-bar" />

<Choice exercise="read-one-bar" />

<OnPass>D over bar 3, and A on its beat 1: a note of the chord, as the first note of a bar usually is.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
The drums keep the waltz. The tune has no chords yet, so its page has no names above it. Give it some, then make it yours.

<Make exercise="your-own-page" />

<Recap />
</Step>
