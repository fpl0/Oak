<Step id="S1">
The four-chord loop again, and a tune over it. Nothing in it bumps where the chords change: listen to how settled it sounds.

<Example id="hook" views="code,roll" autoplay />
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Same chords, same rhythm, two tunes a step apart. One of them fits.

<Choice exercise="which-one-fits" />

<OnPass>Card B moves every note up one degree, and the tune stops agreeing with the chords, most of all at the start of each bar.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Beats 1 and 3 are the strong beats of a 4/4 bar, the ones you would count loudest: ONE, two, THREE, four. Check what the tune plays there.

<Example id="beats-one-and-three" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Every note on beats 1 and 3 belongs to its chord: G and E over C, G and D over G, E and C over Am, C over F. G over the G chord is its root. A melody note that belongs to the chord sounding under it is a **chord tone**. The notes between, such as the F in bar 1 and the D in bar 3, are not, and they do not need to be: they pass on the way to the next chord tone.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Now you check. The chords are the loop's four; the tune is new.

<Choice exercise="in-the-chord-or-not" />

<OnPass>E is in C, and A is in F. A over G and G over Am are not chord tones: on a strong beat, each one leans against its chord.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
This tune lands well in bars 1 and 3. Bars 2 and 4 start on a note outside the chord: hear the bump, then fix it.

<Editor exercise="fix-the-landings" />

<OnPass>B over G and F over F: now the start of every bar agrees with its chord, and the tune sounds as settled as the hook.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
By ear now. Chord tones are the notes to listen for first: on the strong beats, the tune is inside the chord you hear.

<Editor exercise="hear-where-it-lands" />

<OnPass>Degree 5, then 3, inside the C chord; 2 over G, down to 7 below home, then up to home as the C chord returns. 7 and 2 are both notes of G, which is why it pulls so cleanly back to 1.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
The band keeps time and plays the roots; the chords are in your code. Land on them on beats 1 and 3, and use the beats between however you like.

</Step>
