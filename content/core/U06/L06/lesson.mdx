<Step id="S6">
V usually goes home. Sometimes a song sets that up and then goes somewhere else.

<Choice exercise="the-swerve" />

<OnPass>Card B: V to vi. The tune lands where you expected, on the high 1, but the chord under it turns minor and the phrase does not quite finish. V that goes to vi instead of I is a **deceptive cadence**. In the four-chord loop rotated to vi IV I V, that swerve happens every time the loop comes round.</OnPass>
</Step>
