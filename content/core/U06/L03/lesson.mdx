<Step id="S3">
The seven chords of the key fall into three groups by what they do. Run them and listen for rest, motion and pull.

<Example id="the-seven" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Home is I, with vi beside it: they share two notes. iii is drawn there too, but it is the least settled of the three. Away is IV and ii. Tension is V and vii°, which pull towards I. The job a chord does in a phrase is its **harmonic function**. The home job keeps the name tonic, your word for the home note. Leading away is the **predominant** job. Tension is the **dominant** job, named after V, the chord on degree 5.</OnPass>
</Step>
