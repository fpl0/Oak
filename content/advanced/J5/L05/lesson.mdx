<Step id="S2">
Where do the three homes sit? Strip the cycle down to its homes and read the bass.

<Example id="homes" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>B, G, E♭, B: four half steps down each time, three equal steps to the octave. Put each new home's V7 in front of it and you have the cycle of the first step: **Coltrane changes**, after the tunes John Coltrane wrote on them in 1959 and 1960.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Now from C: the ii–V–I you have played since J1, sent on a detour through two other keys before it gets home.

<Editor exercise="detour-through-e" />

<OnPass>Dm7, E♭7, A♭maj7, B7, Emaj7, G7, Cmaj7: three homes a major 3rd apart, A♭, E and C, each reached by its own V7. The ii–V–I is still there at both ends; the middle travels. A good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
