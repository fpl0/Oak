<Step id="S3">
Three chords, read two ways: by their numerals in C major, and by the way the bass moves between them.

<Example id="the-gesture" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>ii7, V7, Imaj7: the minor seventh on 2, the dominant seventh on 5, home on the major seventh on 1. The bass falls a 5th from each root to the next (or climbs a 4th, the same letter), and each chord pulls into the one after it. Heard as one gesture, it is the **ii–V–I**, the sentence most jazz tunes are made of.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
The same gesture in B♭ major. The letters change; the numerals do not.

<Choice exercise="number-it-in-b-flat" />

<OnPass>ii7, V7, Imaj7 in B♭: Cm7, F7, B♭maj7. A good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">

<Editor exercise="home-on-f" />
