<Step id="S3">

<Example id="the-ride" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Six hits a bar. The extra hit inside beats 2 and 4 waits until two thirds of the way through its beat, so each pair is long, then short: ding, ding-a.</OnPass>

<Step id="S4">
The trio needs its cymbal. Four plain beats keep time; two short hits in the right places make it swing.

<Editor exercise="ride-pattern" />

<OnPass>Ding, ding-a, ding, ding-a: that is the **ride pattern**, the swing drummer's timekeeping on the ride cymbal. A good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Jazz audiences snap on two beats of every bar. Find them with the hi-hat.

<Tap exercise="snap-two-and-four" />

<OnPass>Two and four: the beats a swing drummer's foot closes the hi-hat on, and the beats the whole band leans toward.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
The cymbal swings on its own. Put the drummer's foot under it.

<Editor exercise="hats-two-and-four" />

<OnPass>Cymbal on top, hi-hat on 2 and 4: the swing pocket the rest of the trio sits in.</OnPass>
</Step>

The band plays a bass and chords. The drum chair is yours: start from four plain beats and make the groove swing, then make it your own.
