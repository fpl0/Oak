<Step id="S1">
A drummer never hits every sixteenth the same; a loop that does sounds like a machine. The rhythm track starts there, inside the bar. Same groove twice: only the sixteenth notes on the hi-hat change.

<Example id="shaped" views="code,roll" autoplay />
</Step>

<Step id="S2">
Before you count anything, feel where the bar is heavy.

<Tap exercise="tap-one-and-three" />

<OnPass>Beats 1 and 3: the two heaviest places of a 4/4 bar. Beat 1 is the heavier of the two.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S3">
Now read it. The hi-hat plays all sixteen places of the bar, each as strong as the place it stands on.

<Example id="the-levels" views="code,roll" />

<OnPass>Five levels, heaviest first: beat 1, then beat 3, then beats 2 and 4, then the four “ands”, then the eight sixteenths between them. That ranking is **metric weight**: what a listener expects of each place in the bar, whether or not anyone plays it louder.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S4">
Syncopation plays against that ranking. Guess first, then run it.

<Predict exercise="leave-three-empty" />
</Step>

<Step id="S5">
Your turn. A note pulls against the beat when it sits on a light place and the heavier place after it stays silent.

<Editor exercise="pull-against-three" />

<OnPass>One note, one sixteenth early, and beat 3 left to the listener: the bass now leans into the middle of the bar. Notice that the push only works because beat 3 is empty. A good place to stop for today if you need to.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S6">
By ear. How hard a note pulls depends on two places: the light one it sits on and the heavy one it leaves empty.

<Choice exercise="which-pulls-harder" />

<OnPass>The hardest pull is a note on a light place in front of an empty heavy one: the last sixteenth of a bar in front of a missing downbeat pulls hardest of all. A note on an off-beat with the beat after it played does not pull at all.</OnPass>
</Step>

<Step id="S7">
The band plays the chords. The drums and the bass are yours, and the bass sits square on the beat. Make it pull, then make it yours.

<Make exercise="your-bass-pulls" />
