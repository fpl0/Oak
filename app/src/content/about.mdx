# About Oak

Oak teaches music theory by having you write music in [Strudel](https://strudel.cc), a live-coding language for patterns. It runs entirely on this computer: your progress lives in a local database, sounds are served from a local pack, and nothing leaves the machine unless you turn the tutor on.
